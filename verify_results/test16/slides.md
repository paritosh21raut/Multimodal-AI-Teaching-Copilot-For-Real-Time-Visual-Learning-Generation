## Slide 01: Schrödinger's Equation (part 1)
*definition* · Quantum Physics

- **Schrödinger's equation**: A fundamental differential equation used in chemistry and physics to solve atomic structure problems
- Describes particle behavior in electric fields
- Describes change of physical quantity over time
- Schrödinger received Nobel Prize for developing the equation
- Mathematical expression involves energy and position of a particle
- Accounts for wave nature of electrons, protons and atoms
- Based on three considerations, including the classical wave equation

## Slide 02: Schrödinger's Equation (part 2)
*key_points* · Quantum Physics

- Provides detailed form of the wave function (probability waves) that govern motion of small particles
- The equation shows how waves are affected by external factors
- It incorporates energy conservation to describe electron behavior
- Ψ (wave function) and τ help determine the quantum system
- Numbers, orientation, and shape of orbitals where electrons are bound
