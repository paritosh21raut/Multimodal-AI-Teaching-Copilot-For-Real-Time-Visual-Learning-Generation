## Slide 01: Atom
*definition* · Atomic Structure

- **Atom**: the smallest indivisible particle of matter
- Atomic radii: ~10⁻⁸ cm
- Contains three subatomic particles: electrons, protons, neutrons
- Atomic radii are of the order of 10 to the power minus 8 centimeter

## Slide 06: Thompson Model
*members* · Atomic Structure

- **Thompson's atomic model**: The atom is a positive sphere with electrons distributed within the sphere
- Alternative name: Plum coding model
- Explains atomic neutrality
- Cannot explain Rutherford scattering experiment
- [image] Thomson atomic model diagram (CC BY 4.0)

## Slide 07: Rutherford's Nuclear Model (part 1)
*key_points* · Atomic Structure

- Based on Alpha Particle Scattering Experiment
- Atom possesses a highly dense, positively charged nucleus
- The radius of the order 10 to the power minus 13 centimeter
- Mass of the atom is concentrated in the nucleus
- Electrons revolve around nucleus in circular orbits
- Electrostatic attraction holds electrons and nucleus together
- Cannot explain alpha‑particle scattering experiment
- Accelerated charged particles emit electromagnetic radiation, which comes from electronic motion and thus orbits continuously
- Nucleus radius: ~10⁻¹³ cm

## Slide 08: Rutherford's Nuclear Model (part 2)

## Slide 12: Isobars
*concept* · Atomic Structure

- **Isobars**: Species with the same mass number but different atomic number

- **Isobars**: Species with the same isotopic number
- Energy is emitted continuously as radiation and magnetic field
