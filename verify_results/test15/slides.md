## Slide 13: Threshold Wavelength (part 1)
*definition* · Photoelectric Effect

- **Threshold wavelength**: Longest wavelength that can cause ejection of photoelectrons from a metal surface
- Wavelengths longer than the threshold do not produce photoelectrons
- Wavelengths shorter than the threshold cause electron emission
- Thus, if λ is less than λts, then the photolytic effect will take place and eject it in the electron beam
- At the threshold wavelength, emitted electrons have zero kinetic energy
