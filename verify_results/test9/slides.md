## Slide 01: What is digestive system?
*definition* · Digestive System

- **Digestive system**: Also called the gastrointestinal system, it consists of the digestive tract (the gut) and organs such as the liver, pancreas, and gallbladder, and it breaks down food and absorbs nutrients.
- [image] Diagram showing some of the main organs of the digestive system. What are common health conditions of the digestive system? Gut microbiome Digestive system | healthdirect (Your material)
- It is a long, twisting tube from mouth through esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine to anus

## Slide 02: How Digestive System works
*key_points* · Digestive System

- Each organ has an important role in digestion
- Mouth teeth and tongue break food into small pieces
- Saliva moistens food and contains enzymes that start carbohydrate digestion
- This makes it easier to chew and swallow
- Esophagus transports food from mouth to stomach
- Lower esophageal sphincter stops stomach contents from returning to esophagus
- Stomach breaks down food with liquid
- Helps digest and absorb vitamins and minerals

## Slide 03: Small Intestine
*facts* · Digestive System

- Length: ≈5 m
- Structure: Many bends and folds
- Function: Absorbs nutrients and passes them into the blood.
- Most chemical digestion: Occurs in the small intestine

## Slide 04: Large Intestine
*facts* · Digestive System

- Large intestine: Absorbs water, minerals and some vitamins
- [image] large intestine diagram (CC BY-SA 2.5)
- Undigested fiber mixes with mucus and bacteria, breaking down fiber and keeping the large intestine healthy
- Solid bowel motions are formed and stored in the rectum before exiting
- The rectum is the last part of the large intestine before the anus
