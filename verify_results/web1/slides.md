## Slide 07: Compartments (part 1)
*key_points* · Biology

- Compartments are membrane‑bound regions fully enclosed by a lipid bilayer
- Cartoon lines represent lipid bilayers
- [image] cell compartments diagram (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **Compartment**: Completely encapsulated by a lipid bilayer
- Lipid bilayer shows circles as polar head groups and squiggly lines as fatty acid tails
- Lipid bilayer encloses compartments
- Lipid‑bilayer head groups are hydrophilic (water‑loving)
- The water is on sort of the different faces here
- Fatty acid tails are hydrophobic
- Hydrophobic layer surrounds each compartment
- Barrier prevents water and dissolved substances from passing
