## Slide 05: Reproduction (part 3)
*concept* · Reproduction

- **Asexual reproduction**: Plants give rise to new plants without male or female reproductive parts and do not produce fruits or seeds
- **Vegetative propagation**: New plant develops from a portion of the main plant’s body, produced manually or artificially
- **Fragmentation**: New plants are developed from fragments
- **Apomixis**: type of asexual reproduction in which seeds are formed and the embryo develops without fusion of male and female gametes
- Citrus trees use apomixis to reproduce via seeds

## Slide 06: Reproduction (part 1)
*concept* · Reproduction

- **Sexual reproduction**: production of new plants through embryos produced by the fusion of male and female gametes
- **Flower**: the reproductive part of the plant which can be unisexual or bisexual
- **Stamen**: male reproductive part of the flower
- **Pistil**: female reproductive part of the flower
- Sexual reproduction is divided into three categories
