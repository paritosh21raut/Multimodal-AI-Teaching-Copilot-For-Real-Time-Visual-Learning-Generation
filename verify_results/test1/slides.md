## Slide 01: Background
*key_points* · Newton's Laws of Motion

- Sir Isaac Newton (1643–1727) was an English scientist and mathematician who discovered gravity and the three laws of motion.
- 1643–1727

## Slide 02: First Law
*definition* · Newton's Laws of Motion

- **First law of motion**: An object at rest stays at rest and an object in motion stays in motion unless acted upon by an unbalanced force
- Object at rest stays stationary
- Object moving at constant velocity keeps moving
- Force is required to change an object's motion

## Slide 03: Unbalanced Force
*definition* · Newton's Laws of Motion

- **Unbalanced force**: A force on an object when the forces are not equal and opposite
- Balanced forces cause no change in motion
- Unbalanced forces cause motion to change

## Slide 04: Inertia
*definition* · Newton's Laws of Motion

- **Inertia**: the tendency of an object to resist changes in its state of motion

## Slide 05: Second Law
*concept* · Newton's Laws of Motion

- **Newton's second law**: Force equals mass times acceleration; force is directly proportional to mass and acceleration
- **Acceleration**: Measurement of how quickly an object changes speed
- Imagine a ball of a certain mass moving at a certain acceleration
- If mass doubles while acceleration stays constant, force doubles
- Force originates from an object's mass and its acceleration
- Doubling acceleration also doubles force

## Slide 06: Third Law
*definition* · Newton's Laws of Motion

- **Newton's third law**: For every force acting on an object, an equal force acts in the opposite direction
- Gravity pulling down on the object
- Seat pushes up with a force equal to gravity pulling down
- Balanced forces keep you stationary
