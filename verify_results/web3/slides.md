## Slide 05: Derivative Notation (part 1)
*concept* · Mathematics

- **F'(x₀)**: the slope of F at x₀
- **P**: point (x₀, f(x₀))
- Two pieces of information determine a tangent line
- Many lines can pass through a given point
- The tangent line is the one that just touches the curve at that point
- Tangent line touches the curve at exactly one point
- Tangent line touches the curve at only one point and does not cross it at another point Q
- A line may cross the curve many times, but only the tangent touches at exactly one point

## Slide 06: Derivative Notation (part 2)
*concept* · Mathematics

- **Secant line**: Line through two points on the curve
- **Tangent line**: the line that touches the curve at only one point
- **Derivative**: limit of the secant slope as the points approach each other
- **P and Q**: points on the curve
- Secant slope approaches tangent slope as Q tends to P
