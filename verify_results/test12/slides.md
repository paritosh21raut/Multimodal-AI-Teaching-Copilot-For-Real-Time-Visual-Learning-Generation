## Slide 09: Key concepts (part 1)
*facts* · Animals

- Digestive system: Continuous tube and network of organs that break down food and eliminate waste.
- Mechanical digestion: Physical breakdown of food beginning in the mouth.
- Small intestine: Primary site where chemical digestion and nutrient absorption occur.
- Heart: Fist-sized muscular pump with four chambers that circulates blood.

## Slide 10: Key concepts (part 2)
*facts* · Animals

- Arteries: Thick‑walled vessels carrying oxygen‑rich blood away from the heart.
- Veins: Thin vessels returning deoxygenated blood to the heart via the vena cava.
- Pulmonary circulation: Loop that carries blood between the heart and lungs for oxygenation.
- Systemic circulation: Loop that distributes oxygenated blood from the heart to body tissues.
