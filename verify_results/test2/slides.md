## Slide 01: Fluid Mechanics
*concept* · Fluid Mechanics

- **Fluid mechanics**: the branch of physics and engineering that studies the behavior of fluids, including liquids, gas, and plasma, at rest and in motion, as well as their interaction with solid contrast
- Fluid: a substance like water or air that cannot resist shear forces, continuously deforms and flows, unlike a solid that holds its shape
- **Viscosity**: fluid's internal friction or resistance to flow

## Slide 04: Fluid Dynamics (part 1)
*concept* · Fluid Mechanics

- **Fluid Dynamics**: the study of fluids in motion and the forces like pressure, energy and movement that drives that motion
- [example] air passing over an airplane wing; water moving through a pipe
- Key principles and equations
- **Continuity Equation**: Based on conservation of mass, the mass flow rate remains constant throughout a continuous stream

## Slide 05: Fluid Dynamics (part 2)
*concept* · Fluid Mechanics

- **Bernoulli's principle**: Based on the conservation of energy, it relates fluid speed, elevation, and internal pressure
