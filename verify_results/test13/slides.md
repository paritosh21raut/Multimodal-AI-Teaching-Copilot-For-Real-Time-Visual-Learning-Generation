## Slide 02: Human Brain (part 2)
*key_points* · Human Brain

- Interpretation and the origin of control of body movements
- Brain is enclosed within the skull
- Skull provides frontal, lateral, dorsal protection
- Skull has 22 bones: 14 facial, 8 cranial
- Brain constantly contained within the cranium
- Brain surrounded by cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)
- **Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)**: A fluid that circulates within the skull and spinal cord, filling hollow spaces and the surface of the brain; about 500 ml is produced each day by specialized ependymal cells
- Acts as a buffer protecting the brain

## Slide 03: Human Brain (part 3)
*key_points* · Human Brain

- Cushions mechanical shocks and dampens minor jolts
- Provides basic immunological protection
- Gives buoyancy, suspending brain in CSF
- Negates brain weight
- If brain were fully suspended in CSF, its weight would impede blood flow to the lower half, causing neuron death

## Slide 14: Medulla Oblongata
*key_points* · Human Brain

- Small structure in the lowest region of the brain
- Controls autonomic functions such as heartbeat, breathing, digestion
- Connects the spinal cord
- It connects the spinal cord, pons, and cerebral cortex
- Helps maintain posture and controls reflexes

## Slide 15: Pons
*key_points* · Human Brain

- Primary structure of the brain stem between midbrain and medulla
- Relays signals between lower cerebrum, spinal cord, midbrain and higher brain areas
- Controls sleep cycles
- Regulates magnitude and frequency of respiration
- Involved in taste, hearing and balance
- The process of the reciprocation transfers information between the cerebrum and motor cortex
