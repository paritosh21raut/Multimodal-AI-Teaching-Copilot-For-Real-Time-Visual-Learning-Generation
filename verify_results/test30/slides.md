## Slide 01: Semiconductors (part 1)
*definition* · Semiconductors

- **Intrinsic semiconductor**: a pure semiconductor where every atom is the same element
- At room temperature silicon behaves like an insulator due to few free electrons and holes
- Thermal energy creates a free electron‑hole pair in silicon
- Free electron is repelled left by the negatively charged plate and moves orbit to orbit until it reaches the positive plate
- Holes create a new wall at point A, acting like moving original walls right, attracting another valence electron
- Valence electron moves into the hole and can travel left or right

## Slide 17: Forward Bias (part 1)
*key_points* · Semiconductors

- Negative source terminal connects to n-type material

## Slide 20: Reverse Bias (part 1)
*key_points* · Semiconductors

- Negative battery terminal connects to P side, positive to N side creates reverse bias
- Reverse bias widens the depletion layer

- Positive terminal attracts free electrons, pulling them away from the junction

## Slide 34: Zener Diode (part 1)
*definition* · Semiconductors

- **Zener diode**: silicon diode optimized for operation in the breakdown region
- Small‑signal and rectifier diodes are not operated in breakdown because it may damage them
- holds the load voltage almost constant despite large changes in line voltage and load resistance
- Forward voltage = knee voltage + voltage across Zener resistance
- [image] Zener diode (CC BY-SA 4.0)

## Slide 35: Zener Diode (part 2)
*concept* · Semiconductors

- Reverse voltage equals breakdown voltage
- Additional voltage appears across bulk resistance in reverse region
- Voltage stays constant even if current changes
- Normal operation requires reverse‑biasing the diode
- **Zener resistance**: Resistance equal to the inverse slope of the I‑V curve in the breakdown region; a steeper (more vertical) slope means smaller resistance
- Zener diode functions as a general voltage regulator
- **Voltage regulator diode**: A Zener diode that maintains a constant output voltage

## Slide 36: Zener Diode (part 3)

## Slide 46: Optocoupler (part 1)
*key_points* · Semiconductors

- Optocoupler (also called opto isolator)
- LED and photodiode are combined in one package
- LED is on the input side, photodiode on the output side
- Light from the LED activates the photodiode
- Output voltage equals supply voltage minus voltage across the output resistor
- Output voltage varies with input voltage as light intensity fluctuates
- Combination of LED and photodiode is called an optocoupler
- It couples input signal to the output circuit
