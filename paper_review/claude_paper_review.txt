Fix before submitting (quick)
Teacher edits contradict the setup. Section IV-A says "slides were not edited during a test; only two items were deleted", but IV-D says 7 of the 91 changes "were teacher edits". Reconcile the two: say which lectures had edits, or exclude those edits.
The slide-change rates don't match. Table V gives the proposed system 1.25 slides/min, but Table IV gives the identical full planner 1.15 changes/min. Either define the two metrics differently in the captions, or use one metric.
The abstract overstates the guarantee. "Extend-only slide operations that cannot rewrite what students have read" sits next to a 2.0% rewrite rate. Change it to "the LLM cannot rewrite…".
The model citation doesn't match the model. qwen/qwen3.8-27b is cited to the Qwen3 technical report [26]. Check the exact Groq model ID and cite the right report, or say which family it belongs to.
Check the equations in the PDF. In the extracted text, the ≥ / ≤ / fraction signs in Eqs. (1), (3) and (4) come through as "st τ" and "13W(u)". They're probably fine in Word, but look at the PDF to be sure.
Double-blind: if the venue is double-blind, remove the names, emails and affiliation. Also replace "available from the authors on request" with an anonymized repository link.
Weaknesses reviewers will still raise
No append-only LLM baseline. It's acknowledged as a limitation, but it's the obvious question: "would just prompting the LLM to never rewrite give the same 2%?" One extra run (about 0.5 M tokens) answers it. This is the most valuable remaining experiment.
The baseline reports only rewrites, missing statements and boundaries. Add its key-fact coverage and unsupported %. If the LLM-controlled deck also loses taught facts, that strengthens your case further.
The WER comes from a native-English MIT lecture. Your recorded speakers are likely Indian-accented, which is the case reviewers care about. Hand-correct ~10 minutes of one recorded lecture.
The annotators are still authors, and the teacher ratings have no comparison condition. A blinded rating of baseline vs proposed slides by the same five teachers would be a cheap, strong addition.
The gate is not swept, so the 11.2 s latency is justified only in words.
No per-model breakdown (gpt-oss vs qwen).
